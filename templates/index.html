LEVEL 01 COUNT CHECK Awaiting audit...
Simply counts rows in Source vs Destination. If Source has 2,000 but Destination has 1,999 — something was deleted.
LEVEL 02 BALANCE CHECK Awaiting audit...
In double-entry bookkeeping, total debits must equal total credits. If they don't balance, an amount was tampered.
LEVEL 03 CHUNK HASH RADAR Awaiting audit...
Groups rows into chunks of 200 and computes a SHA-256 fingerprint for each. Matching chunks are skipped — only mismatches are examined.
LEVEL 04 BISECTION DRILLDOWN Awaiting audit...
When a chunk has a mismatch, split it in half repeatedly. Finds the exact bad row in ~8 steps instead of checking all 200.
